INTERPRETATION

Hikmah transfer hypothesis

PatchQuestions studies the value of asking distinguishing questions when candidate behavior is known. A repair agent can adapt the principle by identifying competing patches, selecting a legitimate executable distinguishing check, and recording the evidence. The assumption of perfect labels must not be carried into real repositories. Compare end-to-end resolved issues, not only localization quality.
